Pipeline Architecture

How MarketMind Works

From raw transactional CSV logs to actionable business intelligence in four rigorous steps.

1
CSV Ingestion

Upload raw transaction feeds with schema validation, header checking, type verification, and null handling.

2
ETL Transformation

Deduplication, dirty row rejection to staging, surrogate key assignment, and star schema dimension loading.

3
Data Mining Engine

Execute mlxtend Apriori for frequent itemset rules and scikit-learn K-Means for multi-feature RFM clustering.

4
Actionable Intelligence

Interactive OLAP slice/dice cubes, natural language rule explanations, and executive performance reports.
